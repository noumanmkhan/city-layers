Ballot Placement Lottery Results

The Chicago Board of Elections offers a variety of reference materials for candidates and campaigns who are preparing to file to run for elected office, including:

  • Poll sheets that list the voters in specific precincts and voting districts (at the Board's Offices at 69 W. Washington, 6th Floor)
  • Computer terminals for records examination for petition circulators to verify names, addresses, and voting districts of signers (at the Board's Offices at 69 W. Washington, 6th Floor)
  • Maps of precincts, wards, and other voting districts

Candidates must file all campaign finance reports and documents with the Illinois State Board of Elections. Please visit their website by clicking here.

By law, the Chicago Board of Elections cannot provide direction or advice to campaigns on the preparation of petitions and related campaign documents. The Board strongly encourages any and all persons considering a run for public office to consult with an attorney.

Chicago Electoral Board and Petition/Objection Filing Schedule and Deadlines

Notice of Campaign Resources Available to Candidates at the Chicago Election Board

 

NONPARTISAN CHICAGO ELECTED SCHOOL BOARD CANDIDATE PETITION AND OBJECTION FILING SCHEDULE

  • Monday, May 18, 2026: Downtown Voting Supersite, 137 S. State St, 9:00am – 5:00pm

First day of filing for nonpartisan candidates for the offices of President and Member of the Chicago Board of Education (all candidates in line by 9:00am will receive a First Day Filing Ticket for the ballot placement lottery).

  • Tuesday, May 19 through Friday, May 22, 2026: Board Conference Room, 69 W. Washington St., 8th Floor, 9:00am – 5:00pm

Filing for nonpartisan candidates for the offices of President and Member of the Chicago Board of Education at the Board Offices at 69 W. Washington St., 8th Floor.

  • Monday, May 25, 2026: OFFICE CLOSED

Board Offices will be closed for Memorial Day.

  • Tuesday, May 26, 2026: Downtown Voting Supersite, 137 S. State St, 9:00am – 5:00pm

Last day of filing for nonpartisan candidates for the offices of President and Member of the Chicago Board of Education (all candidates filing between 4:00pm and 5:00pm will receive a Last Day Filing Ticket for the ballot placement lottery).

  • Tuesday, June 2, 2026: Board Conference Room, 69 W. Washington St., 8th Floor, 9:00am – 5:00pm

Last day to file objections to nonpartisan candidate nominating petitions.

  • Wednesday, June 3, 2026: Board Conference Room, 69 W. Washington St., 8th Floor, 10:00am

The ballot placement lottery will take place on Wednesday, June 3 at 10:00am at 69 W. Washington Street in the 8th Floor Conference Room. This lottery will determine placement on the ballot for the November 3rd General Election of nonpartisan Chicago Elected School Board candidates who filed simultaneously and received a First Day Filing Ticket (in line by 9:00am on May 18) or a Last Day Filing Ticket (filed between 4:00pm and 5:00pm on May 26). Candidates are not required to be present for this lottery.

 

MUNCIPAL AND ALDERMANIC CANDIDATE PETITION AND OBJECTION FILING SCHEDULE FOR THE FEBRUARY 23, 2027 ELECTION

July 28, 2026 - Petition circulation begins for Municipal and Aldermanic candidates for the February 23, 2027 Election in the City of Chicago.

October 19 through 26, 2026 - Petition filing period for Municipal and Aldermanic candidates for the February 23, 2027 Election in the City of Chicago.

October 27 through November 2, 2026 - Objection filing period for Municipal and Aldermanic candidates for the February 23, 2027 Election in the City of Chicago.
